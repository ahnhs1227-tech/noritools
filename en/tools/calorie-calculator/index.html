Please read

This calculator uses typical serving sizes, average food calories and body-based estimates. Calories vary significantly with preparation, product and actual portion size. Your needs also depend on body composition, muscle, health conditions, medication and sleep. Results are for reference only.

If you're still growing, pregnant, breastfeeding or have an eating disorder, diabetes, kidney disease or other health concerns, don't restrict your diet based on this result. Consult a doctor or nutrition professional.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter food that's not listed?

Check the product's nutrition label or restaurant's calorie information, then enter calories per serving under “Add manually”.

When will I reach my target weight?

Weight change depends on activity, hydration and health as well as food, so no target date is promised. Follow your trend over weeks.

Is my food log saved?

No. Calculations are kept only on this page and disappear when you refresh or close it.

Calculation method and food example values

Resting energy is estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. The maintenance estimate applies an activity factor; the goal example subtracts or adds 300kcal and is not displayed below 1,200kcal. These are simplified tool assumptions, not a personal intake recommendation or a safe minimum.

The food list contains quick-entry example values, not exact matches to specific products or public database entries. Prefer the actual product nutrition label. You can look up the food and serving size in the official food database and enter your own value.

Worked example: enter a nutrition label

If a yogurt label says 80kcal per 100g and you eat 150g, enter 80 as kcal per serving and 1.5 as quantity to record 120kcal. Foods labelled as 0kcal, such as some unsweetened teas, can also be added.
