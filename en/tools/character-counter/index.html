What is the difference between including and excluding spaces?

Including spaces counts spaces and line breaks; excluding spaces counts non-whitespace characters. Submission rules vary, so check your requirements first.

Frequently asked questions

How many bytes is a Korean character?

A Korean syllable usually takes 3 bytes in UTF-8.

Do line breaks count as characters?

The count including spaces includes line-break characters.

Is my text saved?

No. This tool neither saves nor transmits your input.
