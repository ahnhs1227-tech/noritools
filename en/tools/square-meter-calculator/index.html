What does “hebe” mean?

“He be” is a trade term for a square meter (m²). A 1 m × 1 m area is 1 m², about 0.3025 Korean pyeong.

Formula

Width (m) × height (m) × quantity × (1 + waste rate)

Frequently asked questions

How many Korean pyeong is 1 m²?

1 m² is about 0.3025 Korean pyeong; 1 Korean pyeong is about 3.3058 m².

Why add waste allowance?

It allows extra material for cutting loss, breakage and pattern alignment.

What is the unit price based on?
