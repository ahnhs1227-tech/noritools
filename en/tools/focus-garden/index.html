    How timers and the Focus Garden differ

    Timers measure each task and the stopwatch records elapsed time. Neither adds flowers or focus sessions. The Focus Garden adds a flower only when you confirm completing your goal after time runs out. The website cannot determine whether you actually focused.

    Keep your records safe

    Completed flowers are stored in this browser with goals, durations and dates. Earlier daily records and totals are kept too. Older records without goal details do not generate invented flowers. Download your records from My Garden.
