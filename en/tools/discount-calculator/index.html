Extra discounts apply in sequence to the reduced price, and the coupon is deducted last.

Why aren't discounts simply added?

20% off followed by another 10% off saves 28%, not 30%, because the second discount applies to the reduced price.

Example

KRW 50,000 → 20% off → another 10% off = KRW 36,000

Frequently asked questions

Does the coupon apply to each item?

The coupon applies to each item. For a whole-order coupon, set quantity to 1 and enter the full order price.

Is shipping included?

Shipping is excluded. Check shipping fees and minimum order requirements at checkout.

Worked example: coupons and quantities

A KRW 50,000 item discounted by 20%, then 10%, costs KRW 36,000. A KRW 3,000 coupon per item reduces it to KRW 33,000; two items cost KRW 66,000. The two discounts do not add up to 30%.

Order-level coupons need a different input

For two items with one KRW 3,000 coupon on the whole order, the total is KRW 72,000 − KRW 3,000 = KRW 69,000. Enter the original price as KRW 100,000 and quantity as 1. Shipping is excluded.
