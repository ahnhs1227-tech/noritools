Nori Imagine is a collection of quick, playful ways to discover a different you. Today it’s a parallel life; next time, perhaps another unexpected possibility.

Your resident card and life story are free to explore. If you like, move into Eunha Apartments to meet a spouse and neighbors played by real users. Chat in the corridor, shared lounge or bathhouse.

All results are made up for fun. Your original birthday is not sent to the server. This does not assess your real personality or predict your future.
