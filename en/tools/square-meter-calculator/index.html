How area and quantities are calculated

Base area is the sum of width × length × quantity for each space. Required area = base area × (1 + waste percentage ÷ 100).

Round required area ÷ area per piece up to a whole piece. For full packs, round packs up and multiply by the pack price.

Tile example for a 12 m² space

A 3 m × 4 m space with 10% waste needs 13.2 m². For 600 × 600 mm tiles, you need 37 pieces, or 10 packs (40 pieces) when sold in packs of 4.

Waste allows for cuts and breakage. The example's 10% is not a universal recommendation; adjust for your material and site.
