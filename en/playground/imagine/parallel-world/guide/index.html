Welcome to Eunha Apartments, 1988

Your birthday creates a funny portrait, a random Korean name and gender, and a small fictional life story. This is entertainment, not a prediction or an assessment of your appearance or personality. You can just view and save your fictional resident card without moving in.

Choose a neighborhood

Your selected language sets the default server: Korean, English, Japanese or Simplified Chinese. Global welcomes everyone. These are separate communities on shared infrastructure, not servers located in each country. We don’t infer nationality or track your location. Switching display language keeps your home; moving servers is a separate choice. Global offers optional on-device translation.

Free translation in Global

Turn on free translation to read other residents’ chat, speech bubbles and open phone conversations in your display language. Your own messages and saved originals stay unchanged; View original shows the source. Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese are prepared together. For other detected languages, use the message’s enable button. Translation runs on your device in supported browsers, currently desktop Chrome. Unsupported browsers, unclear language or failures show the original. No extra account or translation fee is needed. Language packs may download and use device storage and data. After changing your display language, turn translation on again.

Move in when you’re ready

Up to 12 real players share six homes in each village. We fill the oldest village with space in your chosen server before creating another. A male and female adult character share a home as fictional spouses. If no compatible home is available, you wait in that village’s lounge; a home is assigned when a place opens. There are no fake-player spouses. Use a village code or list to join friends. Full villages cannot accept more residents. Moving ends your old marriage setting and assigns a new home.

Make yourself at home

Click the floor or use arrow keys to walk. Doors lead to corridors, neighboring homes, the lounge and the bathhouse. Sit on chairs or the floor; each chair holds one resident. In the bathhouse your character wears a towel and can use stools and the bath. Messages are visible only to residents in the same place.

TV and radio

Channel 1, Eunha Theater, plays five 52-second melodramas in order: The Secret of Room 101, Mother-in-Law’s Rent Hike, The Suspicious Pink Letter, The Will in the Wardrobe, and The Missing Wedding Ring. Young-su, Sun-hee and Ok-ja have distinct synthetic Korean voices. Subtitles follow your display language, with larger captions below the scene. Channels 3–5 keep the silent retro cartoons. The small TV is unchanged. Power, channel and start time are shared; press Play if autoplay is blocked. Press Play on the radio to start the music, and use the same button to stop. Four original instrumentals play in order, only on your device. They keep playing between rooms and stop on reincarnation or page exit. TV and radio share your personal sound settings and start muted at 15%. No external broadcast or music services are used. The year stays 1988; the date and clock follow current Korean time. Channel 2, Eunha Music, loops four 30-second stages: Eunha Stars, Youth Radio, Neighborhood Patrol Boys, and Rubber Gloves. Group names and song titles follow your display language; songs remain in Korean. Playback follows the shared TV clock, while your TV visibility, mute and volume stay personal. Use the channel buttons on the television to choose a broadcast.

Who gets the remote?

Normally everyone can control the lounge TV. With two or more people, start a contest: 30 seconds to join, then 30 seconds for non-candidates who were present at sign-up close to vote. One vote each, changeable until the deadline. The winner gets 10 minutes of exclusive power/channel control and holds a big remote. Other controls show a lock, the holder and time left; personal sound and TV visibility stay free. Ties, no voters or no votes lead to rock-paper-scissors. Hands stay hidden until all choose or 30 seconds pass. A timeout forfeits that round; if nobody chooses, sharing resumes. Draws repeat; a sole remaining candidate wins. Leaving or reincarnating ends eligibility; disconnection is detected after about 45 seconds. Home TVs are separate.

Stay in touch, or start again

Use the old mobile phone and an 8-digit contact code to save contacts and send private messages, even across servers. Visits require moving to the same server first. Reincarnating resets your character, home and marriage, but preserves your contact code, contacts and retained phone messages. The same birthday and gender produce the same face. Your session lives in this browser; deleting site data or using another device won’t restore it. Village and contact codes are public, not passwords. Don’t share real personal details or pressure anyone into roleplay.
