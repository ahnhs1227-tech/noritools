One photo at a time

Photos are resized by their longest side while keeping their aspect ratio. Small images are not enlarged. Originals stay unchanged; new files get nori added to their names. Duplicate names receive numbers.

Choose a file format

Choose JPG or WebP for smaller photos, or PNG for lossless saving with transparency. Depending on quality and image content, the result may be larger than the original. Animations are saved as one still image. Images up to 40 megapixels are supported.

Organize with peace of mind

Images are processed only in your browser. To reduce mobile memory use, photos are processed one at a time, with up to 120 MB of results retained. You can save completed images even after stopping. Changing size, format or quality clears previous results, so save them first.
