Simple Business Card Editor

Create a clean business card with your name and contact details. Save as PNG and print at actual size.

Free, no sign-upMobile friendly

Your logo replaces Nori on the right side of the card.

Save PNG

The dotted line marks the finished cut size.
The background extends 1 mm past the dotted line. The line is not saved or printed.

Before printing

Select 100% or actual size and turn off headers and footers in the print dialog. With 1 mm bleed on each side, the file and print size is 92 × 52 mm or 88 × 54 mm. PNG files include 300 dpi metadata. Tell your printer the finished size too. CMYK conversion, double-sided alignment and crop marks are not provided. Check your print shop's specifications first.

Contacts and logos stay in your browser

Your name, contact details and logo are neither sent to a server nor stored persistently. Select your logo again after refreshing. Saved PNG files stay on the device you save them to.
