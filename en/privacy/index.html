NORI / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Effective / updated: November 4, 2026

Your input and photos

NORI Tools needs no account. Numbers, text, recipes and uploaded photos are processed in your browser and are not sent to or stored on a separate server for tool use. Work may disappear when you refresh or close the page. Saving, copying and sharing occur only when you choose them.

Browser storage

Your language choice is saved in this browser and is not sent to a server. Translations are included with the site; your input is never sent to an external translation service.

Favorite tool IDs are stored in this device's localStorage and aren't sent to a NORI server. Background-removal models may be cached to avoid repeated downloads. Clear site data in your browser to remove them. Favorites aren't shared across browsers or devices.

External downloads and hosting

The site uses GitHub Pages. Visiting and downloading external files may send standard request details such as IP address, access time and browser information to those services. The background-removal engine loads from jsDelivr, the detailed U²-Net model from Hugging Face, and the fast model from this site. Requests do not include your photos or tool inputs.

GitHub privacy information · jsDelivr privacy information · Hugging Face privacy information

Advertising and analytics

NORI Tools uses Google AdSense. Subject to Google review and ad settings, ads may appear on selected pages such as the home page, guides and playground overview. We have not installed a separate Google Analytics tracking code.

Google and third-party advertising providers may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on previous visits to this and other sites or to measure advertising performance. Ad requests may send information such as IP address, device and browser details, and the visited page to advertising providers.

We do not directly send resume, quotation or signature content, or uploaded photos, as ad-personalization data. Ad scripts are not installed in document and image editors or on the playable game pages.

Advertising and cookie choices

You can manage personalized ads in Google Ad Settings, use third-party advertising choice tools, and control cookies in your browser. Blocking cookies may limit some functionality.

Where consent is required, Google consent messages let you accept or reject the use of information for advertising and manage detailed choices. Messages and available choices may vary by location and applicable settings.

How Google uses data on partner sites · Google Ad Settings · Third-party advertising choices

Email inquiries

Sending an inquiry shares your email address and message with the email service. You need not include sensitive information or original photos. Messages are reviewed only as needed to address your inquiry.

Contact: designmodi@naver.com

Nori Playground records

Game high scores, best moves and times per memory-game difficulty, and sound preferences are stored locally. Choosing to save a record stores your nickname, score (moves and time for Memory), timestamp and record ID on this device's leaderboard. The last nickname is also saved for convenience. Up to 100 players' best records are kept per game/difficulty. Each leaderboard's clear button deletes its records. Nothing is sent to a server or synced across devices. Clearing browser site data also deletes these records.

Sticker Studio, batch photos and Focus Garden

Sticker text, photos and batch-edit images are processed in your browser. Sending a sticker to the layout helper temporarily stores one finished image in this tab's sessionStorage, then deletes it after reading. Nori Timer stores multiple reminder titles, times, spoken text and running status; completed flower goals, times and dates; mode, goals, notification text, time, sound and voice settings; active timers, stopwatch and laps; daily focus records for the last 365 days and total completions in localStorage. Speech uses only voices the browser identifies as on-device; text isn't sent to a separate voice API. Data isn't sent to a server or synced. Delete focus records or browser site data to remove it. Completed flowers remain after their date passes and can be exported.

Business cards, stamps and symbol collections

Names, contact details, uploaded logos, stamp text and collected symbols are processed only in your browser. They aren't sent to a separate server or saved persistently. File saving and clipboard copying happen only when you press their buttons.

The stamp editor loads web fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which may receive standard request details such as IP address and browser information. Your stamp's name or company text isn't included. Google Fonts privacy information

Signature pad

Hand-drawn signature coordinates and images are processed only in the current browser memory. They are not uploaded or automatically saved and disappear when the page is refreshed or closed. PNG saving occurs only at your request.

Exchange rates

Calculating an exchange rate may send selected currency codes and standard connection details to the public Frankfurter API. The entered amount is not sent. Currency-pair rates, reference dates and retrieval times are cached locally for one hour. Choose manual rate entry to avoid connecting to the external service.

Frankfurter service and privacy

Nori Resume

Resume names, contact details, photos and other information are processed in your browser without uploading or automatic saving. Unsaved entries disappear when you refresh or close the page. Choosing Save draft downloads a JSON file containing your entries, original photo, crop settings, tables and display settings to your device. Loading a draft reads your selected file in the browser without uploading it. Draft files contain personal details and photos; you control their storage, sharing and deletion. Printing and PDF saving also happen only at your request.

Saved quotations and transaction statements

Quotation supplier details, line items, logos and stamps are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. If you choose to save, supplier profiles use localStorage and the quotation archive uses IndexedDB in this browser on this device. Delete records in Supplier Management or the quotation archive, or clear browser site data. Back up documents before clearing browser data. PDF export loads conversion software from jsDelivr without sending document contents.
