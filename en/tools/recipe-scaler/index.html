Keep your recipe's proportions

Adjusted amount = original amount × (target servings ÷ original servings). Going from 2 to 5 servings multiplies amounts by 2.5: 200 g becomes 500 g and 1/2 tablespoon becomes 1.25 tablespoons. Results use your chosen decimal places; tiny amounts are shown as below the display threshold, not zero.

Units stay the same

Cup sizes and ingredient densities vary, so g/mL and cups/g aren't converted automatically. Keep the original units and use a scale when needed.

Check seasoning and cooking time separately

Use scaled seasoning as a guide, adding gradually and tasting. Cooking time and heat don't grow in the same ratio as servings. Adjust for cookware size and ingredient thickness. For eggs and other hard-to-divide ingredients, weigh portions or adapt to the recipe.

Recipes aren't sent to a server or saved automatically. Copy your result into your own notes if needed.
