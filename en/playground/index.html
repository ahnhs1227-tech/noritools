What would you like to play today?

Pick Fruit Basket for quick movement, Find a Match for a calm memory challenge, or Run, Nori! to test your timing. Use touch on phones, or mouse and keyboard on computers.

After a game, save a nickname and record. The top three on this device win gold, silver and bronze trophy highlights. Take turns with friends or family on the same device. Records stay in this browser and aren't shared across devices. Sound effects start off and can be enabled in the game. Switching away automatically pauses play.
