Small details, authentic stamp feel

Characters keep a consistent size and original proportions rather than being stretched individually. Oval stamps use vertical names; round personal seals read top to bottom, starting on the right. Square seals support horizontal or vertical text and automatic or manual lines. Business seals place the company name outside and a title inside. Choose Noto Serif KR for Hanja. Unsupported characters may use a device fallback font.

These stamp images are for document drafts, packaging labels and journals. They do not provide certified e-signatures or legal seal registration. Included open fonts recreate stamp layouts. For a specific traditional seal typeface, load a font file you are licensed to use.

Fonts and privacy

Names and company names are processed only in your browser. Fonts load from Google Fonts without an account or paid API. All 21 included web fonts use the SIL Open Font License 1.1, allowing advertising-supported and paid-subscription services. Selling font files by themselves is not permitted. Device fonts and uploaded fonts are subject to their own terms.
