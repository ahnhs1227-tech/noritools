Privacy & how it works

Prototype information, updated November 11, 2026.

Birthday and fictional identity

The original birthday is converted into a number in your browser and is not sent or stored. The face seed, random name and gender, and result identifier are stored. The seed is not encryption and does not guarantee anonymity. Portraits and stories use our own artwork and prepared text, without sending birthdays to fortune-telling sites or generative AI. Cards show only fictional dates and addresses, never a real national ID number. They are not valid identification. Images are saved in your browser; viewing a result alone does not reserve a home or spouse.

Session and storage

A private session key stays in your browser; the server stores its hash and your public contact code. The key accesses your result, contacts and private messages. Shared links contain neither the key nor your birthday. Lost browser data or a different device cannot restore the session. The older GPT-hosted site and Nori Tools have separate browser storage. Nori Tools connects to the game’s separate backend. After moving in, your home, position, seat, activity and public messages are sent to display shared activity. Your face, name, contact code, home and couple’s story can be public. A public contact code cannot log in as you. Your language preference is stored in this browser. TV is visible whenever you open the game; hiding it lasts only for the current visit. Server choices separate communities; no nationality or location lookup is performed.

Messages and reincarnation

Neighborhood chat shows messages from the last 24 hours and since your current character moved in. Private conversations show the latest 50 messages from the last 7 days. Expired records are cleaned on later initial-session or move-in requests, not necessarily deleted instantly. Reincarnation resets your character, home and marriage but keeps your contact code, contacts and messages within retention. Originals are preserved; optional translation runs on your device.

On-device chat translation

If you enable free translation in Global, the browser detects and translates messages on your device. Chat is not additionally sent to a translation server, and there is no paid fallback. The browser provider may download language packs, using bandwidth and local storage. Up to 300 translation results are kept only in page memory and cleared when translation is turned off, the language changes or the page closes. Stored original messages do not change.

External broadcasts

TV power, channel and start time are stored to share the viewing choice. The TV offers ten original Korean-voiced dramas, four music-show performances and three silent animations. Its actors are fixed fictional characters created with the game’s face generator; real users’ names and chats are not turned into video. The video file is served by Nori Tools. The game does not connect to YouTube or another external video service. Hide TV only affects your current visit. Opening the game again starts with TV visible. The radio synthesizes original instrumental scores inside your browser, without external music services, recordings, samples or broadcaster links. Playback and track choices are not sent to or stored on the server. Sound is local to your device and starts muted at 15%, using the TV and radio volume controls. Pressing Stop on the radio, reincarnating or closing the page ends playback.

Remote contests and conduct

The server stores the lounge contest, candidates, voting eligibility, votes and rock-paper-scissors choices. Individual votes and unfinished hands are not exposed to other users. Candidate names, readiness, previous-round results, the holder and expiry are displayed. A new contest or the end of control replaces the prior state. All characters are fictional adults. Don’t post real addresses, phone numbers, IDs or other sensitive data, harass people or impersonate others. Hide messages only hides neighborhood messages on your screen; it does not delete them or block phone messages.

Ads and hosting

The result and chat screens run no first-party advertising, analytics scripts or payment features. Other Nori Tools pages have their own privacy notice. Hosting providers may process network information needed to operate the service.
