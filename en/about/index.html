Easy to find, easy to use

Tools are grouped into Images & Writing, Life & Shopping, Space & Making, and Health & Food. Start photo background and margin edits, price comparisons and print-sheet calculations in one place.

Understand the basis of each calculation

We explain inputs, formulas, examples and limitations alongside results. Explore real cases in the linked guides and try your own values. Health estimates are for reference and do not replace professional diagnosis or dietary prescriptions.

Photos and inputs stay on your device

Current tools run in your browser. Selected photos aren't uploaded to a separate server for editing. Favorites stay on this device. See the privacy policy for external model downloads and hosting details.
