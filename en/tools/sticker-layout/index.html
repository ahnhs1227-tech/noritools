In the print dialog, choose Actual size or 100% and turn off headers and footers. Open downloaded SVGs in software that supports actual-size printing.

Enter dimensions and quantity to see the layout

Enter sticker width and height, paper size, printer margins and cutting gaps. Stickers are arranged in a uniform grid. With rotation enabled, the tool compares a 90° rotated grid and picks the orientation that fits more. It does not optimize mixed orientations.

Example: 50 stickers, 40 × 60 mm

On A4 paper with 10 mm margins, 3 mm gaps and rotation enabled, 18 stickers fit per sheet after a 90° rotation, requiring 3 sheets. Without rotation, 16 fit per sheet and 4 sheets are needed. Only the remaining stickers are shown and printed on the final sheet.

Print at actual size

SVGs and print pages use millimeters. Fit to page may change the size. Use 100% scale and check your printer's paper sizes and printable area. Print one test sheet and measure its width and height with a ruler.

Repeat one design across a sheet or save cutting outlines without a design. Images keep their aspect ratio and fit inside each cell; cutter-specific files are not provided. Print up to 50 sheets at once. For larger batches, save the current-page SVG in separate batches.
