How are units converted?

Values are first converted to a base unit, then divided by the target unit's ratio. One inch is exactly 25.4 mm, so 10 inches is 254 mm.

Example

10 inch × 25.4 = 254 mm

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert inches to centimeters?

Multiply inches by 2.54 to get centimeters.

How do I convert Korean pyeong to square meters?

Multiply Korean pyeong by about 3.305785.

How many decimal places are shown?

Full precision is kept internally; up to six decimal places are displayed.
