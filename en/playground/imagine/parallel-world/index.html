Opening the door to 1988…

Make a birthday avatar. Meet unexpected neighbors.

My Parallel Self turns your birthday into a funny montage character living in a Korean apartment building in 1988. Discover a fictional resident card and a short life story, then move into Eunha Apartments if you feel like staying.

Can I just make a character?

Yes. Create your birthday avatar, see your result, save the image or share it with friends. Moving into the neighborhood is optional.

What kind of random chat is this?

You meet other players as neighbors in a shared village. Residents receive fictional spouse and neighbor roles and chat in homes, hallways and the lounge. This is a neighborhood avatar chat, rather than a service that repeatedly matches you with random one-to-one partners.

Can I join my friends?

Use the same village code while places are available, or save a contact using their personal contact code. Choose Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese or Global communities.
