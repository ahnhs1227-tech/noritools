Easy to start,
fun to run again.

NEW · SPACE BONUS

Ride beyond the stars!

Collect a rocket to fly fast for 10 seconds. Move up and down to dodge planets and meteors.

Finish +200 pointsSpace star +30 points

Mobile: drag up/down anywhere on the game or hold the arrow buttons. Desktop: ↑↓ or W/S.

A collision ends only the flight. Keep your lives and distance, with 1.5 seconds of protection after landing.

Rockets appear along the trail, at least 45 seconds apart. Magnet, shield and fever timers pause during flight.
Gifts in the forest

◇ Shield · Blocks one hit within 6 sec
U Candy magnet · Attracts nearby candies for 3 sec

One more level every 160 m!

  • Lv.1 Logs and low clouds
  • Lv.2 Obstacle chains and block stairs
  • Lv.3 Stepping platforms over spikes
  • Lv.4+ Slide after landing and mixed sections

Jump onto blocks marked ↑. Hitting their sides costs a life. Upper and lower obstacles don't overlap, leaving room to land. Speed increases and breaks shorten the farther you go.

Gifts alternate between magnet and shield at least 26 seconds apart. Magnets last 3 seconds; Fever does not collect automatically.

Star candy: 20 points, 40 during Fever · Distance: 1 point/m · Three lives!
